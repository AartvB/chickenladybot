# ChickenBotOnceADay — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 27 October 2026

## 1. Overview

ChickenBotOnceADay is a Reddit bot that supports daily counting, streak tracking, and community features.

We aim to keep data handling simple and transparent.

## 2. Data Storage

The bot stores its data within Reddit. We do not store user data in a separate external database.

The bot processes Reddit information as needed to provide its features, such as counting posts and maintaining streaks.

Reddit's own privacy policy applies to information processed by Reddit's platform.

## 3. Information Shared with Discord

The bot shares links to new Reddit posts, and the username of the person who made the post, on Discord.

The bot may also retrieve images posted in the official countwithchickenlady Discord server's `#daily-bot-post` channel for use in its Reddit posts.

The bot does not intentionally share additional user information with external services.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The bot relies on Reddit and Discord to provide its functionality. Their respective privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

- [Reddit Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)

## 5. Data Security

Data stored on Reddit is subject to Reddit's systems and security practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security or uninterrupted availability.

## 6. Data Deletion

If a user removes a post, their data will be removed from the bot's tracking systems after 21 days. The bot does not retain user data beyond what is necessary for its functionality.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the bot's functionality or data handling changes.
